Run safely

  • Watch the road, not the screen. Follow traffic rules, and run where it’s safe to run. A route Wake or Apple Maps suggests can be wrong, closed or unsafe.
  • A ghost is a pace to run against, never a reason to push past what feels safe. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
  • SOS and share your start open Messages for you. They don’t contact emergency services, and they need signal and you pressing Send. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Not medical advice

Wake is a running app, not a medical device. Distance, pace, heart rate zones, energy estimates and training plans are estimates from your phone’s sensors and Apple Health, and can be off. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training plan, especially if you have a health condition.
