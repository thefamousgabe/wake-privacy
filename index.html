Wake Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · Developer: Gabriel McKenzie · Contact: thefilthygarments@gmail.com · Terms of Use · Support

The short version

Wake keeps your runs on your phone. There is no Wake account and no Wake server, so we never receive your runs, your location or your Health data. Wake has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only things that leave your phone are map searches, which go to Apple, and whatever you choose to share yourself.

What Wake uses, and why

  • Location: to record your run, draw your route, put your ghost on the map and follow a route you planned. Wake asks for access while you use the app. It keeps recording when the screen locks or you switch apps, and only while a run is going. Your routes are saved on your phone.
  • Apple Health: optional. If you allow it, Wake reads your heart rate, steps, resting heart rate, VO2 max, sleep and weight, and shows them beside your runs. It also saves each finished run or indoor session to Health as a workout, with its distance, route and an energy estimate, so it counts toward your Activity rings. You can turn saving off in Settings, and change what Wake can read in the Health app at any time. Everything else in Wake works without Health.
  • Motion & Fitness: to count steps and cadence, and to offer to start a run when it notices you moving. Worked out on your phone.
  • Photos: only if you pick a profile photo. Wake crops it and keeps it on your phone. It sees only the photo you pick.
  • Music: to show and control your own music during a run. Wake doesn’t copy or send your library anywhere.
  • Notifications: run cues and the reminders you turn on are scheduled on your phone. There is no push server.
  • Lock Screen and widgets: a run in progress shows on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. That stays on your phone.
  • Apple Maps: maps, place search and route planning come from Apple. When you search for a place or plan a route, the search and the area around you go to Apple, not to us. Apple’s privacy policy covers that.

Your Health data

Health data gets extra care, and Apple’s rules for it are strict. Wake follows them:

  • It is shown to you on your phone and never sent to us or to anyone else through Wake.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never used to build a profile of you.
  • Wake doesn’t store it in iCloud or anywhere off your phone.
  • The workouts Wake saves go into your own Health app, where you can see or delete them.

Where your data lives

  • Your runs, routes, shoes, water and food logs, goals, training plan, saved home, guardians and settings are files on your phone.
  • Backups: if you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Wake’s files are included, as they are for any app. Those backups are between you and Apple.
  • Ghosts friends send you are saved on your phone with their name, route and times, so you can run them again. Delete one and it’s gone.

When you share

Nothing leaves Wake unless you tap share, and it goes only where you send it (Messages, AirDrop, Files and so on). We never receive a copy.

  • A ghost file (.wakeghost) carries your display name, the run’s route and times, and any note you wrote on it, so a friend can run it.
  • A share card or a video shows the route and the run’s numbers.
  • Hide home zone when sharing (in Safety): when it’s on, Wake removes the part of a route near your saved home from ghost files, share cards and videos before they leave your phone. If a run never leaves your home area, no route goes out with it.
  • GPX export is a full copy of your run for your own records, so it keeps the whole route, home area included. Keep it private, or share a card instead.
  • Safety: guardians you add (a name and a phone number) stay on your phone. When you tap SOS or share your start, Wake opens Messages with your location filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send. SOS is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Purchases

This version of Wake has nothing to buy. If a later version does, Apple handles the payment through the App Store and we never see your card or Apple Account details. This policy will be updated before then.

No tracking

Wake contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting from other companies, and doesn’t track you across other apps or websites. That’s why it never shows the tracking prompt.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single run from its ⋯ menu.
  • Delete the app and everything Wake saved on your phone goes with it.
  • Workouts Wake saved to Health stay in Health until you delete them there: open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Wake.
  • Ghosts or files you already sent to someone are on their device, and only they can delete them.

We hold none of your data on a server, so there is nothing of yours for us to look up, hand over or delete. If you have a question about your data, email us and we’ll answer.

Children

Wake is not made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If Wake ever sends anything new off your phone, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version ships. The date at the top shows the latest change.
