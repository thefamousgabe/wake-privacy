Wake Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026 · Developer: Gabriel Mckenzie · Contact: thefilthygarments@gmail.com

The short version

Wake is built to keep your data on your device. We do not run servers that collect your runs, we do not sell or share your personal data, and we do not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking. Apart from map and route requests to Apple Maps, anything that leaves your phone leaves only because you chose to share or export it.

What Wake accesses, and why

  • Location (while in use and in the background): to record your run, show your route, run with your “ghost” on real streets, follow planned routes, and finish automatically when you reach a destination. Background access is used only to keep recording an active run so a run is never lost when your screen is off. Your routes are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.
  • Heart rate from Health (HealthKit, read only): if you allow it, Wake reads heart rate samples recorded during a run (usually from an Apple Watch) to show your heart rate zones afterward. This data is read only, is never written back to Health, never leaves your device, and is never used for advertising. Everything else in Wake works without it.
  • Motion & Fitness: to count steps and cadence, and to offer to start a run when it notices you’ve begun moving. Processed on your device.
  • Photo library: only if you choose a profile photo. The photo is cropped and stored locally on your device.
  • Music library: to show and control your own music during a run. We do not collect your music library.
  • Notifications: run cues and optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Wake uses no push notification servers.
  • Apple Maps: maps, place search and route planning come from Apple. When you search for a place or plan a route, that request goes to Apple, not to us, and Apple’s own privacy policy covers it.

How your data is stored and shared

  • Your runs, routes, shoes, water and food logs, training plan, saved home, guardians, and settings are stored locally on your device as files. There is no Wake account and no Wake cloud.
  • Safety: the guardians you add (a name and a phone number) stay on your device. When you tap SOS or share your start, Wake opens Messages with your location filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
  • Apple Watch: if you wear a paired Apple Watch, your phone sends it your live run so the watch can show it. That stays between your own devices.
  • Sharing is always started by you. When you share a run as an image, a GPX file, or a .wakeghost file, it goes only where you send it (Messages, AirDrop, Files and so on). Wake never receives a copy.
  • Hide home zone: when turned on, Wake removes GPS points near your saved home on your device before a run is shared, so a shared run doesn’t reveal where you live.
  • Export: you can export any outdoor run as a standard GPX file at any time. Your data is yours.

Purchases

This version of Wake has nothing to buy. If a future version offers a purchase, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. We never receive your card details, and Apple’s own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Tracking and analytics

Wake includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

Children

Wake is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. If a future version adds optional cloud sync or social features, we will update this policy before those features collect anything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at the same URL.
