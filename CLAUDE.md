# Wake site

The public privacy and support pages for Wake: `index.html` and `support.html`. What these pages say about the app has to match what the app does, line by line.

Wake is a running app, never a game: no game, chase, race or arcade wording here. Its App Store name is Wake: Outrun Yesterday.

## Studio rules

These come from Gabe, founder of Vile King Studio, in his own words on 23 Nov 2026. They hold for every app, site and store listing the studio makes, and they win over habit. Every Claude Code session in this repo follows them.

1. **Finish the work.** Don't half ass anything. Build it the whole way to ship ready, with every tool in reach: build it, run the tests, look at the screen. A draft is not done.
2. **Names and descriptions are short, reticent and unique.** Familiar and simple, never common, never redundant, never complicated. A simple metaphor or euphemism is good; "Chowbella" is the model. The goal is the most memorable name at the top of its niche.
3. **Natural, necessary words only.** Say it the way a person would, and only what the screen needs. No AI jargon and no filler: no "seamless", "unlock", "elevate", "supercharge", "AI powered", "journey", "effortless", "level up", "game changer". No slogans. If a screen needs a sentence to explain itself, fix the layout instead.
4. **Not everything needs a label. Back buttons do.** Icons, numbers and layout can carry meaning without a caption. A back button always says where it goes or says "Back": never a bare chevron, never an empty back title.
5. **Borders only when they help.** Space, type and fill separate things first. A stroke earns its place only where it does a job, such as a focused field, a selected tile, or contrast on a busy background.
6. **Congruent across the studio.** Same voice, same restraint, same finish in every app. A fix made in one app is a rule for all of them.
7. **Made for Gen Z and millennials.** The point lands in the first second. One idea per screen, few taps, nothing to read twice. Names, subtitles and keywords use the words people actually search for. The style is current and clean, never corporate and never trying too hard.
8. **Make smart calls.** Settle the small things yourself and keep moving. A name, or a design change people will see, goes to Gabe as one short question with your pick.
